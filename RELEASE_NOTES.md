# NBA 2K17 MyTEAM v1.1.0

Updated September 27, 2026.

- Includes 2,170 available cards with all current saved custom cards, artwork, and reusable injection data (40 more available cards than v1.0.0).
- Preserves original card PNGs and editable custom-card packages; no image recompression.
- Adds color-matched HOF, Gold, Silver, and Bronze badge counters plus cyan Open 3PT ratings to draft choices, including final Diamond/Amethyst rounds.
- Updates headband and headband-logo data and the saved Rasheed Wallace source data.
- Sets all Julius Erving cards to face ID 2301 and the Nets Beasts card to jersey 32.
- Corrects David Lee's saved face/cyberface and portrait IDs to 1285 using a stable live roster capture.
- Corrects the custom 90 OVR Kevon Looney card to C/PF and accepts position labels regardless of capitalization or surrounding spaces.
- Includes refreshed screenshots, source code, matching build resources, and SHA-256 checksums.

Download **NBA.2K17.MyTEAM.zip**, extract the entire archive, and run **2K17 MyTEAM.exe**. Keep the included `.project` folder beside the EXE. The build-resources download is optional and only needed to build from source.

For an existing installation, follow [UPDATING.md](UPDATING.md). Extract into a new folder and preserve your previous installation until your personal cards and lineups have been checked.

Compatibility remains limited to verified NBA 2K17 executable/roster layouts. External modded face and portrait assets are not included. The David Lee correction has verified captured IDs and encoding; renewed in-game visibility is not claimed as tested.
