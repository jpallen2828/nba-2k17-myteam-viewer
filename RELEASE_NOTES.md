# NBA 2K17 MyTEAM v1.6.0

- Faster startup with an unpacked runtime and a local catalog cache. The first launch after a catalog or app change prepares the cache. Keep the `_myteam_runtime` and `.project` folders beside the EXE.
- Card Studio now includes **USA Basketball** and **Dream Team** in the **Team logo** menu.
- The archive includes the latest custom cards, including Anthony Edwards and Edrice Adebayo. The Pink Diamond Giannis Antetokounmpo card now shows the correct spelling.
- Opening a saved Blacktop lineup keeps its existing autosave. Includes fixes for custom player appearance and for closing MyTEAM while a game connection is stalled.

Download **NBA.2K17.MyTEAM.zip**, extract the entire archive, and run **2K17 MyTEAM.exe**. Existing users can follow [the update guide](https://github.com/jpallen2828/nba-2k17-myteam-viewer/blob/main/UPDATING.md) to bring over their cards and lineups. **NBA.2K17.Build.Resources.zip** is for building from source.
